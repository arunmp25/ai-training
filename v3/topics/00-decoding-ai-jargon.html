The big picture

An AI system is a car. The model is only the engine.

Every piece of jargon belongs to one of six layers. Click a layer to see what it does and which words live there.

⏱ 3 min

🎤 Say This is the one picture to remember. An AI system is a car, and the model is only the engine. Everything else, the knowledge, the tools, the driver, the brakes, is built around it, and every buzzword lives in one of these six layers.

  • This is the most important slide of the course. Everything else hangs on it.
  • Walk through the six layers in order: Model → Context → Knowledge → Action → Autonomy → Trust.
  • Stress: an engine alone goes nowhere. Most AI disappointment comes from expecting the engine to be the whole car.
  • Ask: “When someone says ‘we’re adding MCP’, which layer is that?” (Action.)
