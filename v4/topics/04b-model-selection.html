Choosing a model: big, medium or small?

There is no single “best” model. There is the right model for each job, chosen on quality, speed, cost and context size, and checked on your own examples.

⏱ 30 sec

🎤 Say Every request we have seen so far went to a model. But which one? Providers offer large, medium and small models at very different prices. Today: how to choose.

  • Link back: the desk (Topic 5) costs money per token, and different models charge very different amounts per token.

The line-up

Most providers offer three sizes

The names change, but the pattern holds. Click each tier. Prices are Anthropic’s list prices per million tokens (November 2026).

⏱ 3 min

🎤 Say Most providers sell a large, a medium and a small model. Anthropic calls them Opus, Sonnet and Haiku. OpenAI and Google have their own equivalents. Bigger means more capable, slower and more expensive.

  • Prices and model names change often. Check current pricing before presenting.
  • Open-weight models (such as Llama, Mistral or Qwen) are an option when data must stay on your own servers, at the cost of running them yourself.
The checklist

Five things to compare

Click each card to flip it.

⏱ 3 min

🎤 Say Quality, speed, cost, context size and features. You rarely get the best of all five, so decide which matters most for this job.

  • Speed matters most for live chat; cost matters most at high volume; quality matters most when mistakes are expensive.
  • Data location and compliance can rule a model out before anything else.

A dial inside one model

Before you switch models, try the effort dial

Many models can think for longer before answering. More thinking means better answers on hard problems, but slower replies and more tokens.

⏱ 2 min

🎤 Say Most current models have a thinking or effort setting. Low effort answers quickly; high effort works through the problem first. Sometimes a medium model at high effort beats a large model at low effort, and costs less.

  • In the API this is a setting on the request (for Claude, the effort level). In chat apps it is often a toggle such as “extended thinking”.
  • Thinking tokens are billed as output tokens.

Takeaways

What to remember

⚖️ Right size for the job

Large, medium and small models trade quality against speed and cost. No single model is best for everything.

💸 Tokens are the bill

Cost = requests × tokens × price. Output tokens cost more, and volume multiplies everything.

🧪 Test, then choose

Run your own examples, set a bar, and pick the cheapest model that passes. Route the hard cases up.
