# Session 3: Slido warm-up poll

Three general questions to gauge the room before Session 3 (agents, harnesses, customising and safety). They are not about the topic content, so there are no right answers.

## 1. Which of these have you used?
*Multiple choice, pick all that apply*

- A chat assistant (ChatGPT, Copilot Chat, Claude, Gemini)
- AI built into a tool I already use (email, documents, IDE)
- An AI that takes actions for me (books, edits files, runs tasks)
- I've built or set up something with AI myself
- None yet

## 2. When an AI does a task for you, how much do you check its work?
*Single choice*

- I check everything, line by line
- I spot-check the important bits
- I trust it unless something looks off
- I haven't handed a real task to AI yet

## 3. What worries you most about letting AI act on its own?
*Single choice*

- It makes mistakes without telling me
- It touches data or systems it shouldn't
- I can't see or explain what it did
- It costs more or takes longer than expected
- Nothing much, I'm comfortable with it

## Presenter tips
- Question 1 shows how much hands-on experience the room has.
- Questions 2 and 3 show how people feel about trust and risk, which leads into Topic 18 (Safety, trust and evals).
- Show the results of Question 3 again at the end of the session and ask whether anyone's answer has changed.
