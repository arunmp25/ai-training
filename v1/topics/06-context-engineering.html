Topic 06

Context engineering

Prompt engineering is about the right words. Context engineering is about putting the right information on the desk, and keeping the wrong information off it.

⏱ 30 sec
  • This is where the industry focus shifted: once prompts are decent, quality depends on what you feed in.
The bigger picture

Your prompt is one slice of the context

In a real AI app or agent, the context is assembled from many sources for every single request. Someone (or something) has to decide what goes in.

Context engineering = designing the system that decides, for every request, which information goes on the desk, in what form, and what is left off.
Good context is:
  • Relevant: only what this task needs
  • Fresh: up to date, not last year’s policy
  • Consistent: no contradicting instructions
  • Compact: summarised where possible
  • Structured: clearly labelled sections
