2 · Inside the engine Deep dive: Day 2

It predicts the next token, one at a time

Text is cut into tokens (≈ ¾ word). The model scores what comes next, picks one, adds it, and repeats. Press auto-play.

⏱ 5 min
  • Key consequence: it predicts what’s plausible, not what’s true. That’s why it hallucinates.

8 · MCP Deep dive: Day 3

MCP: USB-C for AI

One open standard for plugging systems into any AI app. Build a connector (server) once, use it everywhere.

⏱ 3 min
  • Without a standard: N apps × M systems integrations. With MCP: N + M.
