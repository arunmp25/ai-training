They said They meant
“It ran out of context.” The chat got so long that the earliest messages no longer fit on the model’s desk.
“Just RAG it.” Find the right pages in our documents first, then hand them to the model with the question. An open-book exam.
“Put it in a vector database.” Store text by meaning, not by spelling, so a search for “King” can also find “Queen”.
“Give it a tool.” Let the model ask our app to do something, like fetch a live quote. The app does the work, not the model.
“Wrap it in MCP.” Build the connector once, in a standard shape, so every AI app can plug into it. USB-C for AI.
“Let the agent handle it.” Plan, act, check the result, repeat, until the job is done. Usually with a person keeping an eye on it.
“Watch out for prompt injection.” Text hidden in a document or web page can try to give the model new orders. Treat it as data, not commands.

That is seven terms. There are a few dozen more, and they all fit together into one picture.
