Copilot Chat quick wins

Twelve short demos that use only Copilot Chat, with no Copilot inside Word, Excel or Outlook needed. Each one takes 3–5 minutes and comes with a ready-made sample file, prompts to copy, and the point to make.

Before the session

Ten minutes of checks saves a stalled demo.

🌐 Open Copilot Chat

  • m365copilot.com in the browser, signed in with your work account
  • or the Microsoft 365 Copilot app
  • or Copilot in Teams

✅ Check your tenant

  • Can you upload files (➕ or 📎 in the chat box)?
  • Is Create agent available (quick win 12)?
  • Is image creation switched on (quick win 10)?

🔒 Data rules

  • Use only the sample files below in the demos.
  • All names and numbers in them are fictional (HarborShield).
  • Remind people what your organisation allows them to upload.
