Topic 10

The agentic loop

An agent is a model using tools in a loop, deciding its own next step, until the goal is done. That’s the whole idea.

⏱ 30 sec
  • A common definition: “an LLM using tools in a loop”. Everything else is detail.
Two patterns

Workflow or agent?

Both use AI and tools. The difference is who decides the next step.

⏱ 2 min
  • Many “agents” in production are really workflows, and that’s fine. Predictable is good.
  • Use an agent when you can’t write the steps down in advance.
Watch it think

Plan → Act → Observe → Done? → repeat

An agent investigates a broken report. Step through or auto-play and watch the loop go round.

⏱ 4 min
  • Notice it didn’t know the plan in advance. Each observation changed the next step.
  • Loop 3 needed approval to edit a file. The harness enforces that (Topic 11).
  • Loop 4 is verification. Good agents check their work instead of claiming success.
Your turn

You’re the agent: what’s your next move?

Goal: “Book a meeting room for 8 people tomorrow at 10:00.” Tools: search_rooms, book_room, send_invite. Answer in order.

⏱ 3 min
  • The last question is the important one: handle errors honestly. Blind retrying causes infinite loops; claiming success is a “fake success” failure.

Takeaways

What to remember

🔁 The loop

Plan → act (tool) → observe the result → done? If not, go round again.

🛤️ Workflow vs agent

Fixed steps when you know them; an agent when you don’t. Pick the simplest that works.

🎚️ Dial up gradually

Limits, checkpoints and verification make loops safe.
