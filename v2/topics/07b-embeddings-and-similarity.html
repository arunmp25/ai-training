Embeddings: meaning as numbers

To search by meaning, a computer needs a way to measure it. An embedding turns any text into a list of numbers. Cosine similarity tells us how close two of those lists point.

Facts worth knowing
• Typical sizes: 384, 768, 1,024, 1,536 or 3,072 dimensions.
• Same input + same model = same vector, every time.
• Multilingual models put “reset password” and “Passwort zurücksetzen” close together.
• Embedding is fast and cheap compared with generating text.

Measuring closeness

Cosine similarity: do two arrows point the same way?

Think of each vector as an arrow from the origin. Cosine similarity looks only at the angle between them: same direction = 1, unrelated (90°) = 0, opposite = −1. Drag the arrow tips.

Cosine similarity
⏱ 4 min
  • Try “Same direction, longer”: cosine stays 1.00 even though B is twice as long, while the distance changes. Cosine ignores length, which is why it suits text: a long paragraph and a short sentence about the same thing should still match.
  • Real embeddings have 1,536 dimensions, not 2, but the maths and the idea are identical.
Worked example

Calculate cosine similarity yourself

cos(A, B) = (A · B) ÷ (|A| × |B|). Multiply matching numbers and add them up (the dot product), then divide by the two lengths. Edit any number.

⏱ 3 min
  • Dimensions: [animal, pet, vehicle]. Walk the “puppy vs dog” row: 0.9×0.8 + 0.8×0.9 + 0×0.1 = 1.44, lengths 1.20 and 1.21, so cos ≈ 0.99.
  • Ask someone to change “dog” into a truck (0, 0, 0.9) and watch the score collapse.
  • A vector database does exactly this, just with 1,536 numbers and millions of chunks, very fast.
Similarity metrics

Cosine, dot product, or distance?

Vector databases ask which metric to use. Click each one.

⏱ 2 min
  • Practical answer: use what your embedding model’s documentation recommends, usually cosine.
  • Most modern models output normalised vectors (length 1). Then cosine, dot product and distance all give the same ranking, and dot product is just the fastest to compute.

Takeaways

What to remember

📍 Embedding = meaning as numbers

A model turns text into a vector of hundreds of numbers. Similar meaning, similar vector.

📐 Cosine = same direction?

Dot product divided by the lengths. 1 = same meaning, 0 = unrelated. It ignores length.

⚖️ Same model, tuned cut-off

Embed documents and questions with the same model; tune top-k and thresholds on your own data.
